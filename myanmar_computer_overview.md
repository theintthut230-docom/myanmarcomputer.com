# Myanmar Computer (Myanmar Computer Co., Ltd.)

**Tagline:** Technology for Tomorrow / Empowering Myanmar Through Technology  
**Website:** `myanmarcomputer.com`    

---

## Overview

Myanmar Computer is an IT services and technology organization established to support digital infrastructure, IT education, and web technology in Myanmar. It serves as a hub for professional training, internet service setups, corporate IT solutions, and ICT event organization across the country.

---

## Core Offerings & Services

### 1. IT Education & Training
* **Practical Training:** Offers computer training courses designed to equip students with industry-ready skills.
* **Course Options:** Ranges from basic computer literacy and advanced web development to networking and technology infrastructure.
* **Instructors & Facilities:** Certified instructors providing hands-on training in state-of-the-art learning spaces.
* **Corporate Training:** Custom training solutions tailored for businesses and organizations.

### 2. Web Development & Digital Solutions
* **Web Services:** Custom web design and application development.
* **Hosting Packages:** Domain management, hosting, and web maintenance packages.

### 3. Technology Infrastructure & Services
* Cyber cafe setup and management support.
* Hardware sales and business IT maintenance.
* ICT park collaboration (MICT Park in Yangon and Mandalay ICT Park developments).
